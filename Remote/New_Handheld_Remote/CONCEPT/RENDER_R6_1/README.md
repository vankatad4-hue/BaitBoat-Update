These drawings are visual aids based on the R6.1 enclosure concept. The connection image is a block diagram, **not** a pin-level schematic. The CAD previews and STL exports are in the parent CAD folder/workflow artifacts.

### R6.1 concept features shown
- Central 4.3-inch touch display, dual joysticks
- Two button banks arranged 2 columns × 4 rows (16 total); four release controls occupy the upper 2×2 of the right bank
- Dedicated OK and power controls
- Two rear antenna exits, removable rear cover and grip pads
- Internal projection of Waveshare ESP32-S3 display board, carrier PCB, proposed 1S4P battery arrangement, nRF24 primary radio and E22 backup radio

### Still provisional
Battery cell capacity/pack implementation, protection/charging, power rails, component footprints and orientations, exact pin assignments, connector and wiring routing, thermal/safety design and enclosure fit require validation. The image generator's promotional sheet is an aesthetic concept and may contain inaccuracies; use the parametric CAD and engineering documents as the controlling references.
