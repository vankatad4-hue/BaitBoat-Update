# Flytec Remote Mechanical R5

Prototype envelope 310×210×68 mm. Two-piece front/rear shell, sculpted grip silhouette, central 4.3-inch screen aperture, twin joystick apertures, two 2×4 button banks, OK and power keys, two antenna feed-throughs.

Right-bank top four controls are independent: R1 H-L (left bait hopper), R2 H-R (right bait hopper), R3 K-L (left hook), R4 K-R (right hook). R5–R8 are non-actuating utility keys. No combined release shortcut.

Release key hold 1.2s, release, then OK hold 0.8s within 4s. Timeout, STOP, lost authenticated primary link, unknown interlock or cancel aborts. LoRa backup cannot release. Boat must enforce pulse time, duplicate suppression and STOP/output-off.

CAD is a fit prototype; exact board, joystick, switch, battery, antenna, cables and screw dimensions are unmeasured. Print and test fit before finalizing. PETG/ASA, 0.2mm layers, four walls are suggested starting settings, not validated.
