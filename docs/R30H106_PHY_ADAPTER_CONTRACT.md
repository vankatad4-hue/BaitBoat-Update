# R30H-106 — Physical adapter contract
Boat NRF24Link and Remote Sc18is606NrfLink expose ready, txPending, lastTxSuccess, update, sendFrame, receiveFrame. Both sendFrame results mean admission, not application acknowledgement. Single outstanding request adapter retains ticket and generation until pending clears or driver becomes unready. Failure on loss of readiness is conservative. An outstanding TX is polled even if its handoff becomes quarantined; no new traffic on quarantined link.
E22UartLink differs: update clears pending based on AUX and unverified estimated airtime; no public completion state and no verified RF success. sendFrame and receiveFrame call update internally, UART writes may be partial/blocking, duty denial distinct from busy, no explicit pending timeout. Do not substitute UART admission or AUX high for RF success. Need bounded transport service, explicit completion semantics and measured duty/airtime before adapter activation.
Remote nRF uses SC18IS606 I2C-SPI and MCP23017 CE. I2C arbitration and vendor touch ownership unresolved. Driver calls may perform multiple synchronous bus operations. Existing RF signoff and upload gates remain. Critical queue classification remains an application responsibility; do not classify unverified RX as STOP or permit movement commands through critical queue. nRF adapter is compile-only and not constructed in production.
