# Boat board integration R4 — SUPERSEDED historical candidate

**Do not build from this revision.** Owner decision restores the Heltec-only MDD20A design; use [R5](BOAT_BOARD_INTEGRATION_R5.md). The companion-controller proposal below is retained only as historical engineering evidence.

Selected architecture remains Heltec Wireless Tracker V1.2 + UC6580, GY-91 MPU-9250/AK8963, MDD20A, external nRF24 and E22, modular carrier. No hardware has been purchased or inspected for this step.

## Revision evidence and GPIO allocation
Manufacturer's older revision log explicitly documents V1.0 and V1.1, and its download directory provides V1.0/V1.1 resources; V2 is a different design. A V1.2-specific manufacturer schematic/pin map was not located. The R4 CSV lists conditional candidate Tracker signals based on the older revision family; it is NOT an approved netlist. In particular GNSS supply enable GPIO3, shared SX1262 SPI 9/10/11, nRF GPIO4/5, I2C GPIO6/7, E22 UART17/18, E22 M0/M1 15/16 and AUX37 all require exact V1.2 cross-check. 18 may have an LED, 15/16 may be oscillator pins, 37 may be board-specific control; resolve these before wiring. Native SPI must explicitly initialize on verified pins and keep the SX1262 NSS high with MISO released. No GPIO macros enabled. Do not connect the 3S pack to the onboard single-cell charger/battery input.

A direct-GPIO approach needs 4 motor signals, 4 coil signals, WDI, arm and multiple measured feedback inputs in addition to radios and sensors. It cannot presently be responsibly frozen. **Recommended candidate** is a small separate safety-I/O controller such as RP2040 on the modular carrier, communicating via I2C with Tracker. This is a new component proposal, not a confirmed purchase decision. R4 CSV proposes GP0/1 I2C, GP2/3 PWM, GP4/5 DIR, GP6–9 one-hot coil controls and GP10 onward safety feedback. GP labels are silicon GPIO, not connector physical pin numbers. Exact RP2040 board/module, carrier link CRC/sequence/lease protocol and firmware, reset states and physical pinout must be engineered and tested before adopting it. If exact Tracker resources permit a safe direct topology, re-evaluate it instead. Neither topology authorizes enabling the current firmware.

## Sensor wiring and software
- UC6580: verify GNSS power/baud and exact V1.2 routing; UART2 candidate 33 RX/34 TX. Read-only NMEA acquisition supports checksum-validated GGA/GSA/RMC/GST, same UTC epoch coordinates/accuracy/speed, actual 3D fix status and bounded age; repeated same-time records cannot renew age. GGA/GSA/RMC default output may not include GST: use matching receiver protocol documentation to verify and enable GST on the actual module. No undocumented configuration commands issued. Until GST or another verified accuracy report arrives, positioning is invalid for autonomy. 2*sqrt(sigma_lat²+sigma_lon²) is a provisional conservative error estimate, not a certified protection level; HDOP is not converted to metres. Test receiver reboot/time discontinuity and actual output logs.
- GY-91: 3.3V I2C SDA/SCL/GND, AD0 depending on breakout. MPU9250 0x68/0x69 (WHO_AM_I 0x71), AK8963 0x0C (WIA 0x48); initialize bypass, read fuse sensitivity adjustments, 16-bit continuous 8 Hz, reject overflow/overrun. Raw accel and mag diagnostic readings; hard/soft iron scale/mount orientation, magnetic declination and interference calibration mandatory. Tilt-compensated body FRD heading only after calibration; rejects implausible acceleration/field/tilt. No full gyro fusion yet; BMP280 is optional atmospheric sensor, not sonar. Sensor readings never assert local safety, stationary, geofence or battery-healthy flags.
- Candidate pack measurement: ADS1115 at 3.3 V, ADDR grounded (0x48), A0 single-ended, 47.0k/10.0k 0.1% resistors (5.7 divider), 100nF at ADC node, protection sized for real pack transient behavior and unpowered ADC. At 12.6 V pack: 2.211 V ADC, divider current 221 uA; at 15 V transient: 2.632 V. At 3.3 V VDD, any pin must remain 0..VDD despite +/-4.096 V PGA. External input protection and transient/rail-off validation required. ADC single-shot 128 SPS, LSB 0.125 mV, nominal pack resolution 0.7125 mV; accuracy is not resolution. Calibrate gain/offset with meter. Unknown battery state of charge remains sentinel 255, not a linear invented percentage. Battery health thresholds and BMS/load sag profile still need actual measurements.
- Use 100kHz I2C initially; address conflicts and pull-up capacitance checked on exact modules. Candidate 4.7k pullups to 3.3V only after counting onboard pulls. Code caps I2C timeout at 10ms per transaction and GNSS work at 256 bytes/poll. Measure actual loop/radio/ADC latency and serial backlog; independent output watchdog remains required.

## Motor and four-channel solenoid logical connection plan
MDD20A: J_MOTOR_CTRL DIR_L/PWM_L/DIR_R/PWM_R/GND; left 550 motor to M1A/M1B, right to M2A/M2B. PWM low = brake, DIR selects polarity, 3.3 V logic compatible; candidate 20 kHz 10-bit PWM, calibrated duty cap and reversal deadtime. Driver controls do not confirm shaft stop; measured current/rotation required. MDD20A separate mount (88.9x78.74 mm does not fit 77x79 carrier). Motor currents and pack 20A BMS, wiring, fuse and thermal limits must be coordinated; driver rating is not pack rating. Guard physical test buttons.

Four keyed two-pin coil connectors: J_SOL_LH/J_SOL_RH (bait), J_SOL_LK/J_SOL_RK (hook). Coil plus individually fused SOL_12V; negative to dedicated low-side MOSFET drain; source to high-current return. Gate through driver/resistor, defined OFF with pulldown; coil flyback path rated for measured current/energy and required release time. 3S runs from discharged range to 12.6V; coil tolerance unknown. Regulated 12V branch if required. Select MOSFET by Rds(on) at actual gate supply, SOA, current and thermal rather than headline current. Reported design assumption 6A per coil is not measured. Off/idle current feedback needed on each channel; no pulse length or fuse finalization until actual testing. Hardware one-hot gating prevents overlap independently of software. Never run motors and coil simultaneously.

## Independent electrical watchdog candidate
TPS3431 at 3.3V, 100nF bypass; CWD 150pF C0G 5%, SET1/EN held enabled by hardware, WDI falling edge from complete validated loop only (target <=25ms between edges, no autonomous timer pet). 10k pull-up on open-drain WDO. Typical nominal 66.61ms per TI equation; approximate ideal-cap factor bounds with 5% capacitor 59.8–73.5ms, before layout/temperature/propagation and actual switch/current-decay behavior. Need measured total cutoff <=100ms, not just WDO assertion. ENOUT/rail-good, active-low WDO, physical STOP and independent overcurrent/thermal trip feed asynchronously fault-latched actuator gate; latch powers up disabled, reset by explicit deliberate local arm with good rails, outputs off, safe feedback. WDO pulse must not re-enable power when it releases. Gate isolates high-current motor and solenoid rails (separate branches); choose rated normally-off switch/contactor after measured motor stall/current and coil inductive energy, with appropriate transient/recirculation path and protection. Do NOT make the Tracker or companion firmware the only cutoff. Gate/driver fault feedback independent of commanded outputs. Add independent maximum pulse/one-hot gate. Schematic and PCB not finalized; use vendor reference and verify actual tolerances, fail-safe boot, open wire, regulator loss, stuck WDI and forced firmware hang with supply disconnected first.

## Source and tests
`tracker_gnss.h`, `carrier_sensor_math.h`, `tracker_sensor_port.*` implement real register/UART acquisition and portable math. `carrier_output_plan.h` converts signed normalized motor requests to candidate 10-bit PWM/DIR, checks reversal delay and one-hot release transitions; invalid requests latch output off until explicit safe reset; not connected to GPIO. Diagnostic firmware includes a locally supplied `tracker_commissioned_config.h` only when provided after audit, derived from `tracker_sensor_profile.example.h`; prints GNSS/calibrated battery/heading and raw accel/mag data via serial. Default unassigned build does not start sensor hardware or actuators. Actual companion firmware and independent power-gate schematic/PCB remain design work.
